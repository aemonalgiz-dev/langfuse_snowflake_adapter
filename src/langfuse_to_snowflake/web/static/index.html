Filters changed

Only records that match every filter are loaded. Field names are Langfuse's, such as environment, userId or metadata.tier. Several values separated by commas mean any of them.

Fields to leave out changed

A field listed here is removed from every record before it is loaded, so it never reaches Snowflake. Use a dotted name for one key inside a field, such as metadata.email. Filters still see the field.

Sampling changed

A trace is kept or dropped as a whole, with its observations, scores and comments, and the same traces are chosen on every run. Totals in Snowflake then cover the sample only.

Observation content changed

Untick a group to keep it out of Snowflake. Leaving out inputs and outputs keeps prompts and completions in Langfuse only.

Langfuse shortens metadata values over 200 characters unless the key is listed here.
