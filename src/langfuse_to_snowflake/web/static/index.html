    Settings

    What is loaded from , and how often. Changes apply from the next run.

    Changes made here are not saved anywhere, so they last only until the service restarts. Engineering chooses where they are kept, with SYNC_STORE.

    Data Modified

    Each kind of record becomes a table or a view in Snowflake.

    Filters Modified

    Only records that match every filter are loaded. Field names are Langfuse's, such as environment, userId or metadata.tier. Several values separated by commas mean any of them.

    No filters: every record is loaded.

    Fields to leave out Modified

    A field listed here is removed from every record before it is loaded, so it never reaches Snowflake. Use a dotted name for a key inside a field, such as metadata.email. Filters still see the field.

    Nothing is left out: every field is loaded.

    Load Field Type Has a value in Notes

    Sampling Modified

    A trace is kept or dropped as a whole, with its observations, scores and comments, and the same traces are chosen on every run. Totals in Snowflake then cover the sample only.

    Observation content Modified

    Untick a group to keep it out of Snowflake. Leaving out inputs and outputs keeps prompts and completions in Langfuse only.

    How often new records are loaded, and how often what is already loaded is compared with Langfuse again.
